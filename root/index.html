Our Mission Statement:

The purpose of this organization is to rescue, provide veterinary care, foster, transport, and place homeless, abandoned, neglected, abused, surrendered, or at-risk dogs and cats into permanent homes; to promote responsible pet ownership, spay and neuter, vaccination, humane treatment of animals, and community education regarding animal welfare. The organization may also assist with low-cost or subsidized spay/neuter services, vaccination assistance, and other animal welfare support services for underserved or low-income community members as resources permit.
