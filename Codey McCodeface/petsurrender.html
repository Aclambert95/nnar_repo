I certify that the information provided on this form is true and complete to the best of my knowledge. If I am the owner or authorized agent, I voluntarily surrender the animal described above to No Nonsense Animal Rescue (NNAR) and transfer any ownership interest I have in the animal to NNAR upon acceptance by an authorized NNAR representative. I understand that surrender is permanent unless NNAR agrees otherwise in writing.

If I am a finder/community member and not the legal owner, I certify that I have disclosed that status and provided truthful information regarding where the animal was found and my efforts to locate an owner and/or contact the appropriate shelter or animal-control resource. I authorize NNAR to take custody of the animal for rescue purposes subject to applicable law and any required hold, ownership, or transfer procedures.

I understand that completion of this form alone does not require NNAR to accept the animal. Once accepted, NNAR may arrange veterinary care, foster placement, transfer to another qualified rescue, and/or adoption based on the animal's needs and available resources.
