I understand that food distributed by No Nonsense Animal Rescue may be donated from warehouses, retailers, manufacturers, rescue partners, or other donation programs. NNAR cannot guarantee the freshness, storage history, brand, formula, packaging condition, or continued availability of donated food. I will discard and not feed any food that appears questionable.

By accepting donated pet food, I voluntarily assume responsibility for determining whether the food is appropriate and safe for my pet. To the fullest extent permitted by law, I release and hold harmless No Nonsense Animal Rescue, its directors, officers, volunteers, fosters, donors, and partners from claims, liability, illness, injury, loss, or expense arising from the storage, condition, selection, or use of donated pet food, except where liability cannot legally be waived.
